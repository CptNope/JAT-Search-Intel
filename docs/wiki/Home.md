# JAT Search Intel

**SEO, answer-engine and AI-search optimization for WordPress**: meta tags, one connected schema graph, sitemaps, llms.txt, redirects and live SEO / AEO / GEO content scores. Everything runs on your own site. There's no account and no tracking, and no outside requests unless you switch on an optional integration.
